# Vonar 1.0.0

The first release of **Vonar**, a free wavetable synthesizer plugin (VST3, Windows x64) by **Xami**.

## Download

- **`Vonar-1.0.0-Setup.exe`** – installer with a setup wizard (recommended). Installs the VST3 plugin into
  `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3`, where every DAW looks for it.
- **`Vonar-1.0.0-Windows-x64.zip`** – the same plugin without an installer: copy the `Vonar.vst3` folder into
  `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3` yourself.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer is not signed with a paid certificate.
Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's inside

- Two wavetable oscillators, sub and noise, filter, envelopes, LFOs and a modulation matrix.
- An effects rack with 10 effects (distortion, chorus, phaser, flanger, filter, EQ, pump, delay, reverb,
  compressor). Drag effects to change their order; adjust EQ, filter, reverb and compressor right on the graphs.
- **327 factory presets** in categories (including drums for house and hip-hop) and moods.
- Four macro knobs, a level meter and an oscilloscope always visible.
- Low CPU use; effects go to sleep when nothing is playing.

## Coming from VOLT?

The plugin was called VOLT during development. The installer removes the old `VOLT.vst3`, and projects made
with VOLT open in Vonar with their sounds. Your own presets are copied over automatically.
